Language guide

How Tagalog Academy handles canonical forms, alternatives, register, and pronunciation.

A consistent beginner standard

The Academy teaches one canonical form per record and clearly labels common, formal, native, descriptive, and regional alternatives.

Deterministic practice

Every recall prompt identifies one teaching record. When two forms share a meaning, the exercise label explains the usage difference.

Accepted variants

Common spelling and usage variants may be accepted during recall while the lesson continues to display one clear teaching form.

Pronunciation

Browser speech depends on voices installed on the device. Playback supports listening and repetition; fluent speakers and high-quality recordings remain the best reference for pronunciation.

Language corrections

Lessons follow a consistent beginner standard maintained by Tagalog Academy. Evidence-based corrections to wording, meaning, register, spelling, and regional context are welcomed and reviewed through the structured language-correction process.
