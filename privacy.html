Your learning data

Privacy

A simple explanation of what Tagalog Academy stores and what remains under your control.

What is saved

Your lesson progress is saved in this browser. You may export it as a JSON backup and import it on another device.

Voice recordings

Microphone recordings remain in the active browser session. Tagalog Academy does not include an audio-upload path.

Accounts and tracking

No account is required. The application includes no advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking script.

Your control

You can export, import, or remove saved progress from the Use section.
